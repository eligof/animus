# The Startup and Office Life Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A sixteenth world, the Startup (`office`): a startup loft with its own costume on every species (a zip hoodie over a tee, jeans and sneakers; a snapback worn backwards at ★2) and an office dog (a corgi); and office life in every world (a kick-off huddle round a lead that starts subagents, coffee runs, high-fives when a subagent finishes, stand-ups when a crew waits), with an `o` key that turns it off; shipped through a PR.

**Architecture:** Groundwork first, in shared code, test-first with complete code (Tasks 1–2: the costume, the corgi). Then two tracks in parallel worktrees: the Startup's art from a brief (Task 3, one agent that owns only the world's own files and runs no git; the coordinator reviews, commits and registers it in Task 4), and office life with complete code (Tasks 5–8: the new poses, the carried mug and the speech mark; the four moments in `src/world/life.ts`; the `o` key; the demo crew), rebased onto the world. Then the coordinator checks both in a real Ghostty (Task 9), brings the README to sixteen worlds with an "Office life" section (Task 10) and ships through the CI loop (Task 11).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript run directly by Node 26 (type stripping, no build), `node:test`, the zero-dependency rasterizer in `src/art/` (a Canvas2D-like `Pen`, isometric helpers, a PNG encoder), kitty graphics in Ghostty.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-office-design.md`. Art direction and the `Pen` contract: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-illustrated-design.md`. The world conventions this plan builds on: "Conventions for every world" in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-game-worlds.md`, as changed by "Changes after review" in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-game-worlds-design.md`.

**Decisions where the spec meets the code:**

1. **The ★2 hat is a new prize, `snapback`, not the den's `cap`.** Every
   costume has a hat of its own (the hat test counts them), and the den
   already wears a cap backwards. The snapback reads as its own: a tall,
   structured crown in the character's colour with a button on top, a
   flat charcoal brim, and (from the front, worn backwards) the strap's
   opening over the brow with the hair showing through.
2. **The hoodie is a zip hoodie, open over a white tee, in a heathered
   shade of the character's colour**, unlike the den's dark pullover; its
   hood lies down its back (a hooded agent, a security reviewer, wears it
   up, as in the den). Jeans with turned-up hems, white sneakers with a
   heel tab in its colour (no stripes, no swoosh). ★8: the hoodie in the
   trim's hue (as most costumes) and black jeans.
3. **Islands.** `run`, `web` and `think` stand on the ring round the hub
   and `idle` in front of it in every layout (`ISLANDS` in
   `src/world/layout.ts`), free-standing; `write`, `read`, `search` and the
   `entrance` back onto the walls. So the spec's wall-mounted dashboard TV
   is a big TV on a rolling stand, the whiteboard wall a big rolling
   double-sided whiteboard, the server rack free-standing with the 3D
   printer on a cart beside it, and the espresso counter a kitchen island
   with stools.
4. **The neon squiggle and the pendant lamps are the shell's**, flat on and
   along the walls and glowing (the overlay can make the neon buzz), like
   the café's windows (decision 6 of the game-worlds plan); free-standing
   pieces are decor. The decor still makes at least twelve pieces in a
   150 x 46 terminal (the test asks).
5. **Speech marks** are a new emote, `speech`: a balloon with three dots,
   no words, over the lead of a huddle and over each speaker at a stand-up.
6. **The sip.** The existing `drink` pose already lifts a mug in one hand
   (at the worlds' drink spots and in workers' breaks). The new `sip` pose
   holds the carried mug in both hands. A carried mug shows in five poses
   (`MUG_POSES`: standing about, walking, pondering, sitting, chatting),
   always in the same hand; poses that need both hands (typing, reading,
   hammering, a high-five) put it down for the moment. A worker carrying
   one sips at its station now and then.
7. **"The yard or a plaza"**: a stand-up rings the yard (`yard` in
   `src/world/layout.ts`: open floor in front of the hub, kept clear of
   decor; the plazas are where builds go up); one stand-up in the room at a
   time. **"A crew"** is `AgentInfo.crew` (a project): agents without one
   never stand up.
8. **"A subagent's first task is already running"** means its activity is
   not `think` when it walks in (a new live subagent is `think`, "spinning
   up", until its transcript's first record). **Given work** means its
   activity changes (`Life.changed`): it leaves an office moment for its
   station at once (a new `LifeHost.back`), and the stage holds a
   huddling newcomer's station until the huddle ends (`Life.huddled`).
9. **The demo crew, changed deliberately (its pinned hash is updated in
   Task 8 with the reason):** subagents spin up for 6–9 s (was 1.5–3.5 s),
   long enough to walk in to their lead for a huddle; on every other wait
   of a lead (its first, third, …) its running subagents wait with it
   ("waiting for atlas"), so a crew has three or more waiting: a stand-up.
   The busy share stays over 75 % (78.7 % over fifteen minutes). With the
   new moves nova's lead passes two checks before its first finished turn,
   so it starts at 485 XP (was 490) and that turn still brings ★4. And
   `test/world.test.ts`'s card test now measures from the character's whole
   box: with the new moves the card goes above atlas, clear of its ★10 hat,
   which the old measure (from the top of the head) took for docked.
10. **`o` shares the lighting line of the help** ("l / o  lighting / office
    life on / off"): one more line would push `quit` off the help on the
    smallest screen (60 x 20; the help test).
11. **Frequencies.** A huddle at most once a lead every 30 s; a coffee run
    at most once an agent every 150 s, likeliest early in a wait (a mug for
    45–75 s, a sip every 6–12 s); a stand-up at most once a crew every
    180 s, one in the room at a time; a high-five for every subagent that
    finishes while its lead is in the room and awake. With the demo crew
    all four come within three minutes in every world (measured on the
    stage of a 150 x 44 terminal: in the mines a huddle at 8 s, a stand-up
    at 34 s, a high-five at 36 s and a coffee run at 62 s; in the café 7,
    35, 37 and 56 s); over ten minutes in the café (a 150 x 46 terminal,
    two leads and up to seven subagents), 23 huddles, 11 coffee runs, 66
    high-fives and 6 stand-ups.
12. **Coffee where there is no drink spot.** Coffee runs go to a world's
    drink spots, but some rooms have none open (the shelter's sits under
    its decor at 150 x 44; most 80 x 24 rooms lose theirs): there they go to
    a warm spot (the hearth, the stove, the fire), which every world keeps
    from 120 x 33 up (the room tests ask); a room with neither has none. A
    stand-up may call an agent off a coffee run, and a lead about to be
    high-fived is left alone (no stand-up, no chat) until it has been.

**The no-brand rule (the spec's), for every task, file, comment, commit and
picture:** nothing copies a real company's logo, product or brand. No
fruit, swoosh, bird, stripes-in-threes, interlocking rings, overlapping
circles or letter marks; no readable text or digits anywhere in a picture
(screens, boards, sticky notes and the door's mark show lines, bars and
shapes); laptops and monitors plain, no logo on the lid; the espresso
machine, the printer, the bike and the fridge unbranded; the sneakers with
no side stripe; the corgi is just a dog.

// Contact sheets: each costume on every species, front and back, plain,
// with its level's hat (★2), in its other colours (★8) and both; and the
// critters idling and on the move (each a row idle, a row moving; the last
// two frames flipped). Usage:
//   node sheet.ts <src dir> <out dir> [costume,costume,…] [s] [critter,critter,…]
import { writeFileSync } from 'node:fs';
const SRC = process.argv[2];
const OUT = process.argv[3];
const { figureSprite, critterSprite } = await import(`${SRC}/art/figure.ts`);
const { Canvas } = await import(`${SRC}/art/canvas.ts`);
const { encodeRgb } = await import(`${SRC}/art/png.ts`);
const { traitsFor } = await import(`${SRC}/world/character.ts`);
const INK = '#2b2233';
const S = Number(process.argv[5] ?? 7);
const SPECIES = ['human', 'robot', 'cat', 'alien', 'ghost'];
const ids = new Map<string, string[]>();
for (let i = 0; i < 3000; i++) {
  const t = traitsFor(`agent-${i}`, 'sub');
  const l = ids.get(t.species) ?? [];
  if (l.length < 8) l.push(`agent-${i}`);
  ids.set(t.species, l);
}
const who = (sp: string, n = 0) => traitsFor(ids.get(sp)![n], n % 3 === 0 ? 'lead' : 'sub');
const fig = (tr: unknown, costume: string, pose: string, o: Record<string, unknown> = {}) => ({ id: 'x', x: 0, y: 0, z: 0, traits: tr, costume, pose, poseT: 0.3, phase: 0.2, facing: { back: false, flip: false }, selected: false, hurt: false, sleeping: false, walking: false, item: [90, 170, 230], ...o });
const NONE = { hat: false, badge: false, outfit: false, aura: false };
const VARIANTS: Array<[string, object]> = [['plain', NONE], ['hat', { ...NONE, hat: true }], ['outfit', { ...NONE, outfit: true }], ['both', { ...NONE, hat: true, outfit: true }]];
const BG = 0xd9d2c4;
function sheet(costume: string, pose = 'idle'): void {
  const cw = Math.round(17 * S);
  const ch = Math.round(25 * S);
  const cols = VARIANTS.length * 2;
  const c = new Canvas(cols * cw, SPECIES.length * ch);
  c.fillStyle = '#d9d2c4';
  c.fillRect(0, 0, c.width, c.height);
  SPECIES.forEach((sp, r) => {
    let col = 0;
    for (const back of [false, true]) {
      for (const [, wear] of VARIANTS) {
        const spr = figureSprite(fig(who(sp, r % 2 ? 2 : 1), costume, pose, { wear, facing: { back, flip: false } }), S, INK);
        const x = col * cw + Math.round(cw / 2) - spr.ax;
        const y = r * ch + Math.round(ch * 0.9) - spr.ay;
        c.fillStyle = (col + r) % 2 ? '#d2cab9' : '#ddd6c8';
        c.fillRect(col * cw, r * ch, cw, ch);
        c.drawImage(spr.img, x, y);
        col++;
      }
    }
  });
  writeFileSync(`${OUT}/costume-${costume}${pose === 'idle' ? '' : '-' + pose}.png`, encodeRgb(c, BG, 2));
}
const list = (process.argv[4] ?? 'startup').split(',');
for (const co of list) sheet(co);
// Poses in action, one row a costume (human, robot, cat, alien, ghost cycling).
{
  const poses = ['walk', 'write', 'read', 'run', 'search', 'web', 'think', 'sit', 'carry', 'cheer', 'dance', 'sleep'];
  const cw = Math.round(17 * S * 0.75);
  const ch = Math.round(25 * S * 0.75);
  const c = new Canvas(poses.length * cw, list.length * ch);
  c.fillStyle = '#d9d2c4';
  c.fillRect(0, 0, c.width, c.height);
  list.forEach((co, r) => poses.forEach((pose, k) => {
    const spr = figureSprite(fig(who(SPECIES[k % 5], 1), co, pose, { sleeping: pose === 'sleep', wear: k % 3 === 0 ? { ...NONE, hat: true } : NONE, facing: { back: k % 4 === 3, flip: k % 2 === 1 } }), S * 0.75, INK);
    c.fillStyle = (k + r) % 2 ? '#d2cab9' : '#ddd6c8';
    c.fillRect(k * cw, r * ch, cw, ch);
    c.drawImage(spr.img, k * cw + Math.round(cw / 2) - spr.ax, r * ch + Math.round(ch * 0.85) - spr.ay);
  }));
  writeFileSync(`${OUT}/costume-poses.png`, encodeRgb(c, BG, 2));
}
// Critters, if asked: argv[6] a comma list of kinds.
if (process.argv[6]) {
  const kinds = process.argv[6].split(',');
  const bodies: Record<string, number[]> = { corgi: [217, 138, 60], beetle: [196, 120, 62], hopper: [178, 132, 236], husky: [138, 146, 158], cat: [232, 145, 58], fox: [226, 120, 48], rat: [150, 140, 130] };
  const cs = S * 1.6;
  const cw = Math.round(13 * cs);
  const ch = Math.round(11 * cs);
  const frames = 8;
  const c = new Canvas(frames * cw, kinds.length * 2 * ch);
  c.fillStyle = '#d9d2c4';
  c.fillRect(0, 0, c.width, c.height);
  kinds.forEach((kind, r) => {
    for (const moving of [false, true]) {
      for (let i = 0; i < frames; i++) {
        const cr = { kind, body: bodies[kind] ?? [200, 120, 80], x: 0, y: 0, z: 0, flip: i >= 6, moving };
        const t = moving ? i * 0.05 + 0.01 : i * 0.3 + 0.01;
        const spr = critterSprite(cr, t, cs, INK);
        const row = r * 2 + (moving ? 1 : 0);
        c.fillStyle = (i + row) % 2 ? '#d2cab9' : '#ddd6c8';
        c.fillRect(i * cw, row * ch, cw, ch);
        c.drawImage(spr.img, i * cw + Math.round(cw / 2) - spr.ax, row * ch + Math.round(ch * 0.85) - spr.ay);
      }
    }
  });
  writeFileSync(`${OUT}/critters.png`, encodeRgb(c, BG, 2));
}
console.log('ok');

// Office life's poses on every species: the high-five and the sip through
// their loops, then a carried mug standing about, walking, pondering,

const co = process.argv[4] ?? 'startup';
const S = Number(process.argv[5] ?? 6);

for (let i = 0; i < 3000; i++) {
  const t = traitsFor(`agent-${i}`, 'sub');

c.fillRect(0, 0, c.width, c.height);

  cells.forEach(([pose, poseT, mug, back], col) => {
    const tr = traitsFor(ids.get(sp)![1], 'sub');

  });
});
writeFileSync(process.argv[3], encodeRgb(c, 0xd9d2c4, 2));

// blaze, muzzle, chest and paws, a fluffy white rump with a short tuft of a
// tail that wags; it trots on the move, pants now and then at rest.
function corgi(sk: Sketch, f: F): void {
  const a = f.u * TAU;
  const step = f.moving ? Math.sin(a) : 0;
  const bob = f.moving ? -0.16 * Math.abs(Math.cos(a)) : 0.05 * Math.sin(a);
  const wag = f.moving ? 0.35 * Math.sin(a * 2) : 0.5 * Math.sin(a * 2);
  const white = mix(f.b, WHITE, 0.88);
  const whiteD = shade(white, -0.14);
  // the tuft of a tail, wagging over the rump
  sk.at(mul(move(-2.75, -1.95 + bob), turn(-0.6 + wag)), () => {
    sk.blob(ell(-0.35, -0.2, 0.65, 0.42), f.b, { dark: f.bd, d: 0.2 });
  });
  const leg = (x: number, ph: number, near: boolean) => {
    const sw = f.moving ? 0.4 * Math.sin(a + ph) : 0;
    const lift = f.moving ? Math.max(0, Math.cos(a + ph)) * 0.3 : 0;
    sk.limb([x, -0.95 + bob, x + sw, -0.32 - lift], 0.7, near ? white : whiteD, { dark: near ? whiteD : null });
  };
  leg(1.55, Math.PI, false);
  leg(-1.75, 0, false);
  const body = ell(-0.35, -1.6 + bob, 2.6, 1.02);
  sk.blob(body, f.b, { dark: f.bd, d: 0.45 });
  sk.draw((p) => {
    // the white belly, chest and rump
    p.save();
    p.beginPath();
    body(p);
    p.clip();
    p.beginPath();
    p.ellipse(0.1, -0.68 + bob, 2.7, 0.5, 0, 0, TAU);
    p.ellipse(1.75, -1.4 + bob, 0.75, 0.95, 0, 0, TAU);
    p.ellipse(-2.75, -1.25 + bob, 0.7, 0.8, 0, 0, TAU);
    p.fillStyle = white;
    p.fill();
    p.restore();
  });
  leg(1.25, 0, true);
  leg(-2.05, Math.PI, true);
  // the head: big ears up, a white blaze between the eyes and down the
  // muzzle
  sk.at(move(2.2 + step * 0.05, -2.7 + bob), () => {
    for (const [x0, x1] of [[-1.05, -0.2], [0.15, 1.0]] as const) {
      const tip = -2.45 + 0.1 * Math.sin(a + x0);
      sk.blob((p) => {
        p.moveTo(x0, -0.5);
        p.quadraticCurveTo(x0 - 0.15, tip + 0.6, (x0 + x1) / 2, tip);
        p.quadraticCurveTo(x1 + 0.15, tip + 0.6, x1, -0.65);
        p.closePath();

      sk.draw((p) => {

        p.closePath();
        p.fillStyle = '#f2b9a6';
        p.fill();
      });
    }
    const head = ell(0, 0, 1.2, 1.05);
    sk.blob(head, f.b, { dark: f.bd, d: 0.35 });
    sk.draw((p) => {
      p.save();
      p.beginPath();
      head(p);
      p.clip();
      p.beginPath();
      p.ellipse(0.4, 0.7, 1.0, 0.6, 0, 0, TAU);
      p.ellipse(0.2, -0.3, 0.2, 0.75, 0.15, 0, TAU);

      p.fill();

      p.quadraticCurveTo(1.75, 0.85, 1.1, 0.85);
      p.quadraticCurveTo(0.55, 0.85, 0.35, 0.6);
      p.closePath();

// Draws `art` alone on a transparent canvas; returns the canvas and the
// screen box of its footprint raised to its height.
function render(art: PropArt, s: number, wall: 'left' | 'right', island: boolean, t: number, active: boolean): { c: Canvas; box: Box } {
  const pad = 6 * s;
  const w = Math.ceil((art.len + art.dep) * s + 2 * pad);
  const h = Math.ceil(((art.len + art.dep) / 2 + art.height) * s + 2 * pad);
  const at0 = frame(project(0, 0, s), wall, 0, 0);
  let box: Box = { x0: Infinity, y0: Infinity, x1: -Infinity, y1: -Infinity };
  for (const u of [0, art.len]) for (const v of [0, art.dep]) for (const z of [0, art.height]) {
    const [x, y] = at0(u, v, z);
    box = { x0: Math.min(box.x0, x), y0: Math.min(box.y0, y), x1: Math.max(box.x1, x), y1: Math.max(box.y1, y) };
  }
  const ox = pad - box.x0;
  const oy = pad - box.y0;
  const at = frame(project(ox, oy, s), wall, 0, 0);
  const c = new Canvas(w, h);
  art.draw({ pen: c, s, at, len: art.len, dep: art.dep, wall, island, t, active, seed: 1234 });
  return { c, box: { x0: box.x0 + ox, y0: box.y0 + oy, x1: box.x1 + ox, y1: box.y1 + oy } };
}

// The box round every pixel painted at least `min` (of 255) opaque.
function painted(c: Canvas, min = 16): Box | null {
  let b: Box | null = null;
  for (let y = 0; y < c.height; y++) {
    for (let x = 0; x < c.width; x++) {
      if (c.data[(y * c.width + x) * 4 + 3] < min) continue;
      if (!b) b = { x0: x, y0: y, x1: x, y1: y };
      else {
        b.x0 = Math.min(b.x0, x);
        b.y0 = Math.min(b.y0, y);
        b.x1 = Math.max(b.x1, x);
        b.y1 = Math.max(b.y1, y);
      }
    }
  }
  return b;
}

// ── points of interest ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
// The defaults (warm either side of the espresso counter, a stool in front
// of it, a look at the dashboards and the shelves, a dance at the hub, the
// dog's spot in the yard) less their drink by the run prop (a server rack
// here): coffee at the espresso counter instead, at either end of it, a
// second stool, and a look out of up to three windows at the city.
export function officePois(L: Layout): Poi[] {
  const { features: fs } = planFor(L.W, L.D, L.H, L.T);
  const pt = (wall: Wall, a: number, out: number) => (wall === 'right' ? { x: a, y: L.T + out } : { x: L.T + out, y: a });
  const slot = (k: SlotKind) => L.slots.find((q) => q.kind === k);
  const at = (q: Slot, along: number, outward: number) => (q.wall === 'right' ? { x: q.spot.x + along, y: q.spot.y + outward } : { x: q.spot.x + outward, y: q.spot.y + along });
  const mid = (q: Slot) => {
    const r = footprint(q);
    return { x: (r.x0 + r.x1) / 2, y: (r.y0 + r.y1) / 2 };
  };
  const out = defaultPois(L).filter((p) => p.kind !== 'drink');
  const bar = slot('idle');
  if (bar) {
    out.push({ ...at(bar, -4, 0), kind: 'drink', face: mid(bar) });
    out.push({ ...at(bar, 4, 0), kind: 'drink', face: mid(bar) });
    out.push({ ...at(bar, -4, 8), kind: 'seat', face: mid(bar) });
  }
  for (const f of fs.filter((g) => g.kind === 'window').slice(0, 3)) out.push({ ...pt(f.wall, f.a, 6), kind: 'view', face: pt(f.wall, f.a, 0) });
  return out.map((p) => ({ ...p, x: Math.round(p.x), y: Math.round(p.y) }));
}

## Office life

![Office life](docs/screenshots/office-life.png)

In every world the crew keeps a little office life, never at the cost of
the work: an agent at work stays at its station.

- **Kick-off huddle.** When a lead starts subagents, the new ones walk in
  to its side and nod along under its speech mark for a moment, then go
  to their stations (at most once a lead every 30 seconds; not when the
  lead is out of the room or a subagent's first task is already running).
- **Coffee runs.** Now and then an idle agent goes to the world's drink
  spot (the espresso counter, the bar, the mess bench; else the fire or
  the stove) and comes back with a mug, which it carries a while, sipping
  from it, at work too.
- **High-fives.** A subagent that finishes walks over to its lead and they
  high-five (a lead at work turns and raises a hand at its desk); then
  comes its confetti.
- **Stand-ups.** When three or more of a crew are waiting, now and then
  they gather in a ring in the open and take turns to talk.

Anyone given work leaves at once. `o` turns it off and on (remembered),
for pure status.
